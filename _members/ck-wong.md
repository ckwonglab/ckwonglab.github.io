Originally from Hong Kong, CK has made his way across three cities: Vancouver, Toronto, and now London, following his research interest in metabolism, endocrinology, and more recently inflammation. After a PhD in Medical Genetics at the University of British Columbia and postdoctoral work on GLP-1 and inflammation, he started the Endocrine Signalling in Inflammation group at the MRC LMS in 2026. He is now interested in understanding how different hormones, including those produced in the gut, influence inflammation. Outside of the lab, he enjoys playing board games and discovering new ones.
